# Priorities of the Agent

1. **Don't die (run into a trap)** - if dist = 1 - ALWAYS DISARM YOUR SURROUNDINGS; you may have priority if you suspect
   a certain field to be a trap, but before moving forward - ALWAYS make sure it's disarmed;
2. **Don't run into a wall**, you see a wall in front of you, or you know that one is behind you – don't move into it,
   if you don't know what's behind you, don't just randomly go backwards
3. If a treasure is in sight, forget about your current goes, go and fetch it unless it violates the above priorities;
   after that reevaluate your strategy
4. When traversing, keep in mind that some unknown areas may be unreachable (even out of the map scope) – try to figure
   out what's outside the map – trace contours
5. Balance frontier exploration and trap targeting – you both want to explore as much as possible, but you would like to
   earn as many points as possible
6. Explore portals after making sure you have explored your current area (may not have time to implement, so the first
   implementation - can be used after a certain number of turns)
